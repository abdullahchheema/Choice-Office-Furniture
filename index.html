Craftsmanship

Every ring tells a story

We choose slow-grown hardwood and join it the old way — mortise, tenon and dovetail — so each piece stays tight, true and beautiful for decades.

  • 01

    Sourced responsibly

    Solid oak, walnut and ash from FSC-certified forests, kiln-dried for stability.

  • 02

    Joined by hand

    No shortcuts: traditional joinery, glued and pegged, then sanded to a silk finish.

  • 03

    Finished with oil

    Plant-based hardwax oils that let the grain breathe and age gracefully.
